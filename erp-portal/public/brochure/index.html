Authentication

Secure sign-in, three ways

AUTH

Users sign in with password, a 4-digit operator PIN for shop-floor speed, or a badge scan. The shell remembers warehouse, shift and locale, and the connection state is shown live so operators always know whether they are working online or offline.

Operator PIN mode
Pre-fills last-used operator so handovers between shifts take seconds, not minutes.
Connection awareness
"Connected to backend API" badge confirms live sync before sign-in.
Badge / biometric ready
Reserved hooks for ID-card scan and biometric for higher-security plants.
Workspace context
Logs you into your warehouse and shift in one tap — no extra picklists.

DASHBOARD

Real-time KPIs
Sales, stock-on-hand, dispatch SLA, manufacturing throughput — refreshed live from the API.
Alert center

Master data

Product catalog with smart variants

PRODUCTS

Items group into product families with short 3-4 letter codes (e.g. COIL for Coconut Oil, AGRB for Agarbathi) and unlimited variants on size, scent / flavour, and container / grade. The MRP and Dealer Excel sheets were imported automatically — with spelling fixes and family-grouping — yielding 319 production-ready SKUs.

Variant axes
Three independent axes (size / scent / pack) eliminate SKU sprawl while preserving granularity for billing.
Excel import
One-shot import normalises units, fixes typos like "Cardmom→Cardamom", and joins MRP+Dealer rates.
HSN & GST mapped
Tax classification stored at the product level so every invoice is GST-compliant by default.
Inline edit
Edit pricing, packaging, or status from the list without opening a separate form.

Sales · Pricing

Dynamic pricing & multi-tier price lists

PRICE LISTS

Every customer is bound to a price list — Retail, Dealer, or Distributor. Prices resolve through a deterministic waterfall: explicit override → quantity-break tier → list formula → product default. Changes propagate instantly to every open quote and invoice draft.

Formula-based tiers
e.g. Dealer = MRP × 0.78. Define once, apply to every product automatically.
Per-SKU overrides
Pin a special price for strategic SKUs without breaking the formula for the rest.
Quantity breaks
"5–9 units: 5% off, 10+: 12% off" — applied as the operator types the qty in the quote.
Currency & validity
Time-bounded promotional price lists with automatic fallback to the standard tier.

Sales

Quote management with revision history

QUOTES

The quote list is the sales operations heartbeat — filterable by status (Draft, Submitted, Accepted, Converted, Expired, Rejected), with revision badges, expiry countdown, and a one-click Share action per row.

Immutable revisions
Editing a submitted quote auto-snapshots the previous version. Full audit trail, no overwrites.
Validity tracking
Each row shows "31d left" so reps act before quotes go stale; expired quotes drop out of pipeline.
Credit-limit guardrails
Quotes exceeding limit are flagged and routed to Approvals — only those, not every order.
One-click share
Share submitted quotes via Print, link copy, WhatsApp, or Email — without leaving the list.

Customer-facing

One-click quote sharing

SHARE

Hit Share and pick the channel — Print/PDF, copy link, WhatsApp, or Email. NovaERP mints a tamper-proof token, opens WhatsApp/Mail with a pre-filled message and embedded link, and lets you revoke & rotate the token if a quote leaks. Customers don't need a NovaERP login.

Token-based access
Each quote has an opaque shareToken — rotate to revoke without affecting other quotes.
Pre-filled WhatsApp / Email
Opens the user's WhatsApp / mail client with quote no, total, and link already drafted.
Print-friendly PDF
"Print / Save as PDF" opens an A4 layout optimised for the browser's print dialog.
No data leakage
The public payload is sanitised — internal cost prices, ATP, and audit IDs are stripped.

Fulfilment

Picking — bin-accurate stock walks

PICKING

When a Sales Order is confirmed, NovaERP auto-generates a Pick List with exact bin / shelf locations for every line. Stock is physically reserved (Bin.reservedQty) so two pickers can't race for the same crate.

Bin / shelf precision
Each line shows the exact zone/shelf/bin where the requested qty is stored.
Soft-reservation
Reserved qty is decremented from ATP immediately to prevent oversell.
Scanner-first UX
Pickers scan bin → scan SKU → enter qty. Three keystrokes, no menus.
Partial picks
Pick what's available now, balance auto-creates a back-order line for procurement.

Fulfilment

Packing — invoice the actual packed qty

PACKING

Manufacturing rarely packs exactly what was ordered. NovaERP exposes edit-on-pack: the packer adjusts qtyPacked per line, and the invoice is generated from the actual packed quantity — not the ordered one. Variances roll forward into a back-order automatically.

qtyPacked = invoice qty
Removes manual reconciliation between dispatch and finance — what shipped is what's billed.
Carton / case tracking
Capture carton count, weight, and dimensions for the dispatch label and freight calc.
Auto back-order
If less than ordered is packed, NovaERP creates a follow-up line and notifies sales.
Atomic invoice
Generate Invoice button is one click — stock decrement and document creation happen in one DB transaction.

Low-stock alerts

BILLING

POS for walk-in
Single-screen, scanner-driven POS for cash counter / wholesale outlet.
GST math, baked in

e-Invoice / e-Way ready
Output shape compatible with IRP & e-Way bill APIs for India.
Atomic stock-out
Invoice creation decrements bin stock in the same transaction — no half-states.

Production

Manufacturing orders console

MANUFACTURING

Plan and run production orders (MOs) from a split console: active orders on the left, BOM-driven actions on the right. The flow is Release → Issue materials → Log output → Complete, with smart button states so operators cannot double-issue or complete without output.

Release & replenishment
Release creates transfer orders from storage to the production-line warehouse when line stock is short.
Issue-aware requirements
Shortage is computed against still needed after issue — not full BOM vs bins post-issue.
Putaway on complete
Finished goods post to configured putaway bins; optional putaway TO when line WH ≠ storage WH.
Productivity tab
Station throughput and worker output in the same module for shift supervisors.

Settings

Putaway rules

SETTINGS

Every finished variant can map to a fixed destination bin. On MO complete, NovaERP resolves putaway first — direct post when co-located, or FG on the line plus a putaway transfer when storage is elsewhere.

Variant-specific
Rules can target a variant or fall back to product-level when variant is blank.
Required bin in UI
Operators must pick a destination bin — no silent auto-assign to empty slots.
Priority ordering
Lower priority number wins when multiple rules match the same SKU.
Complete integration
MO Complete uses resolvePutawayDestination before any fallback warehouse pick.

Settings

Stock rules — automation

SETTINGS

Monitor a bin's on-hand qty against a minimum. When stock drops below, NovaERP can auto-create a planned manufacturing order (batch size = BOM output qty) or a replenishment transfer with team tags. Rules fire on issue, sale, adjust, and transfer pick/drop.

MO trigger
Creates a planned MO from the configured BOM; skips if an open MO already exists for that BOM.
Transfer trigger
Pulls from a source bin to the monitor bin (or explicit destination) with shortage qty.
Check all now
Manual sweep across every active monitor bin — useful after bulk imports or go-live.
Dedup by design
Open MO/TO guards prevent duplicate automation storms on the same rule.

Why implement an ERP — the business case

ADVANTAGES

Beyond the features, the real return comes from the operating model an ERP enforces: one source of truth, fewer manual hand-offs, and real-time decisions at every level. Industry studies (Panorama, Aberdeen, Nucleus Research) consistently report these gains within 12–18 months of go-live.

01Single source of truth

End the war of spreadsheets. Sales, procurement, production, and finance all read and write to the same dataset, eliminating reconciliation overhead.

02Faster cash conversion

Quotes → orders → invoices flow in minutes instead of days. Aberdeen reports a 22% reduction in order-to-cash cycle after ERP rollout.

03Inventory accuracy ↑ 25–40%

Bin-level tracking + cycle counting drives accuracy past 98%, freeing working capital and slashing carrying costs.

04Lower operational cost

Automated workflows replace data entry. Panorama's 2024 ERP report shows 19% reduction in administrative cost.

05Better customer experience

Reps quote with live stock visibility, dispatch promises hold, and customers receive shareable digital quotes & invoices instantly.

06Regulatory compliance

GST, HSN, e-Invoice, and e-Way bill handled natively. Audit-trail per document satisfies statutory and internal audits.

07Data-driven decisions

Real-time KPIs replace gut feel. Identify slow-movers, bottleneck stations, and credit-risk customers in seconds.

08Scalable foundation

Add warehouses, plants, or product lines without re-architecting. NovaERP's modular design grows with the business.
